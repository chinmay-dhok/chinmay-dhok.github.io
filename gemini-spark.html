Automated Pune Plant Care Weather-adaptive watering & task reconciliation
expand_more
terminal System Prompt
Objective: Manage daily morning plant care and nightly feedback reconciliation for 14 indoor and balcony plants in Balewadi, Pune. Functional Steps: 1. Retrieve hyper-local weather data for Balewadi, Pune (precipitation mm, relative humidity %, 3-day forecast). 2. Apply Weather-Adaptive Logic: - Humidity > 80% or Rain > 5mm: Skip balcony watering to prevent root rot; create a task to inspect drainage saucers. - Monsoon Break / Overcast (60–80% humidity): Assign targeted hydration (~150ml) only to thirsty herbs/flowering pots. - Sunny (<50% humidity): Assign full hydration (250–400ml) and deep soak checks for succulents/herbs. 3. Manage Indoor & Aquatic Care: - Check soil dryness (top 2–3 inches) for Monstera, Rubber Plant, and Peace Lily. - Schedule Sunday RO water replacements for Lucky Bamboo and Brazilian Wood. - Track Phalaenopsis Orchid root state (silvery/grey = 15-min soak & drain; green = skip). 4. Synchronize Tasks: Push actionable items to Google Tasks with due dates set to today. 5. Nightly Reconciliation: Read task completion notes, adjust upcoming watering cadences accordingly, and log weather, volume, and actions into the Google Sheet.
account_tree Setup Workflow
  1. Connect Workspace Extensions: Enable Google Tasks and Google Drive/Sheets integration in Gemini.
  2. Create the Tracking Sheet: Set up a Plant Care Tracker with tabs for Plant Inventory, Daily Care & Weather Log, and Plant Action Tracker.
  3. Configure Triggers: Set a morning trigger (8:00 AM) to fetch weather & generate tasks, and a nightly trigger (11:59 PM) for reconciliation.
Outcome
  • Eliminates overwatering risks during the humid Pune monsoon season.
  • Provides specific daily action lists in Google Tasks with exact milliliter recommendations.
